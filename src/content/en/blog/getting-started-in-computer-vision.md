## Why computer vision?

Computer vision lets machines interpret and understand the visual world. This post covers the fundamentals needed to get started: image processing, convolutional neural networks, and reference datasets.

## Practical first steps

1. Understand the basics of image processing (filters, convolutions).
2. Experiment with libraries like OpenCV and PyTorch.
3. Reproduce a simple image classification project before moving on to object detection.
