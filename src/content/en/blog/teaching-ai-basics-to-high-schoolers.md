## Making AI approachable

Explaining artificial intelligence to high schoolers works best when starting from things they already use: content recommendations, voice recognition, photo filters. This post shares the teaching approaches that worked best.

## What worked

- Starting from concrete examples before introducing technical vocabulary.
- Letting students use simple tools to visualize how a model behaves.
- Encouraging questions and critical thinking about AI-generated results.
