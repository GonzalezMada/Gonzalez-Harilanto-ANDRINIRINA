Artificial intelligence is revolutionizing the way we teach science and technology. In this article, we explore the tools and methods that are transforming classrooms.

## Introduction

STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) education is at the heart of 21st century educational challenges. With the advent of artificial intelligence, new opportunities are emerging to make learning more interactive and personalized.

## AI in the Service of Education

AI tools enable the creation of adaptive learning experiences that respond to the individual needs of each student. From educational robotics to adaptive learning platforms, the possibilities are endless.

## Conclusion

Integrating AI into STEM education is not an option, but a necessity to prepare students for tomorrow's challenges.
